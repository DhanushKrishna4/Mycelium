# Evaluation contract and measured limits

`configs/evaluation-v1.json` was frozen before release training. All five models use the same exact evaluation configuration, seeds, event coordinates, and metric code. Checkpoint selection used separate seeds 910031 and 910037 and a disk intervention, never these final trials. Validation foreground uses target alpha >=0.5; final metric foreground uses the frozen alpha >0.1 threshold. Both use equal foreground/background group weight.

Each model contributes 27 trials: eight growth trajectories to 256, four persistence trajectories to 1024 with measurements every 16 steps, nine repair trials (rectangle, slit, edge cut × three schedules), and six stress trials. The 135 results are preserved in JSON, per-measurement CSV, per-trial CSV, and SVGs. The empty-state balanced error is the relative-error denominator, so an empty image has relative error1 and cannot pass. IoU uses alpha>.1. State channels are never clipped before measurement.

Repair starts at 256 and continues 256 further steps. A successful repair requires a pre-damage acceptable state (relative error<=.35 and IoU>=.65), removal of at least 8% of actually occupied cells, and three consecutive acceptable samples after the event (16-step intervals). Recovery time is the elapsed step at confirmation of that three-sample window. Later deterioration is separately recorded as `deterioration_after_recovery` and `retained_recovery`; this is particularly relevant to one ring-persistence trial. Raw error is measured immediately before and after each event. Normalized recovery is not clipped; a negligible denominator produces JSON null. Damage severity uses the measured occupied-cell fraction: <=.5 moderate, >.5 severe.

All three regenerating gallery models pass 8/8 growth and 9/9 repair. Only the ring passes 4/4 long-persistence trials; leaf and rosette pass 0/4. Ring persistence training passes the same aggregate growth/persistence/repair counts as ring regeneration, with one later loss of repair quality. Ring growth-only passes 0/8, 0/4, 0/9 at these evaluation horizons. Its short training objective is not evidence of mature persistence. This single-seed case study does not establish that regeneration training is universally better.

Stress trials include total erasure, a large cut, update probabilities 0.25 and 0.9 (after 256 steps), a persistent slit lesion, and repeated damage. The total-erasure outcome should remain empty; its quality success is false, while `remained_extinct:true` validates the extinction contract. A model may satisfy the relatively tolerant quality threshold around a forced lesion, but cells in the lesion remain zero. No biological obstacle understanding is inferred.

One long live browser comparison also reached numerical failure in the growth-only control near step 1396. That exploratory observation is separate from the frozen135 trials. The runtime stopped, preserving the last accepted state, rather than clipping or silently restarting.

Exact replay is scoped to the specified NumPy version, runtime, PCG64 generator and sampling convention, and compatible floating-point environment. Short-run PyTorch/native NumPy/Pyodide agreement is tested with explicit masks at rtol 2e-5, atol 2e-6; threshold-neighbor fixtures are separate. This does not promise bitwise identity across numerical backends or indefinite trajectory agreement.

Reproduce with `.venv/bin/python -m mycelium_nca.cli evaluate`. This command evaluates supplied artifacts and does not change selection or retrain. Use separate output directories for additional training seeds and studies. Preserve unsuccessful trials and freeze any revised future suite before using it to evaluate new runs.
